 DATASET DESCRIPTION
The Employee Dataset contains data of 500 employees from the company. It includes details such as Employee ID, Department, Age, Salary, Is_Manager, Experience, Join_Date, and Age Group. The dataset is used for analyzing workforce distribution, salary structure, and hiring trends.

 DASHBOARD KPIs 
- Total Employees: 500
- Total Managers: 257 | Non-Managers: 243
- Average Salary: ₹1,70,634
- Average Experience: 21.31 Years
- Total Departments: 5 (Finance, HR, IT, Marketing, Sales)
- Highest Salary: ₹2,93,410

 SLICER USED 
- Department Slicer: Finance, HR, IT, Marketing, Sales - Used to filter all charts and KPIs department-wise. This is an interactive filter for the whole dashboard.

 BUSINESS QUESTIONS
- Which Department has the highest average Salary?
- What is the count of Managers vs Non-Managers, and which Department has the most Managers?
- What is the average Age and average Experience of employees in each Department? Which department has the most experienced team?
- Does Salary increase with Experience and Age? What is the correlation?
- In which year did the company hire the most employees? What is the hiring trend year-wise?
- What is the Salary distribution in the company? Which Department has the highest and lowest paid employees?
- Which Age Group dominates the company? (20-30, 30-40, 40-50, 50+)

 KEY FINDINGS
- Finance department has the highest average salary, while Sales has the lowest average salary.
- Sales department has the highest number of Managers (60), Finance has the lowest (45).
- Overall average Salary is ₹1,70,634 and average Experience is 21.31 years.
- IT is the biggest department with 110 employees (22%), HR is smallest with 87 employees.
- 40-49 age group dominates the company with 170 employees, 20-29 group is lowest with 77 employees.
- There is a strong positive correlation between Experience and Salary.
- Salary difference between Manager (₹1,71,841) and Non-Manager (₹1,69,358) is only 1.4% - very fair pay structure.
- 167 employees are in ₹1.5L-2L salary slab, which is the majority.
- Finance has 48 employees with 30+ years experience - most experienced team.

 TOOLS USED
- Microsoft Excel - Data cleaning, analysis, calculations, and dashboard creation.
- Excel Formulas - SUM, AVERAGE, COUNTIF, AVERAGEIF, IF, MIN, MAX.
- Pivot Tables - Summarizing department, salary, experience, age groups, and manager count.
- Pivot Charts - Visualizing salary by department, age group, hiring trends, and experience.
- Slicers - Interactive filtering of the dashboard by Department and Is_Manager.
- Bar Chart, Pie Chart, Line Chart, For dashboard visualization.
